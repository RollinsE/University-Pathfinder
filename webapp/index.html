    The 8 buildings it knows
      How it works and credits

      A YOLO11n detector, trained on photos of eight Talbot Campus buildings taken by day and at night, finds and labels the buildings in your photo. It runs in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web. Two classifiers, ResNet18 and MobileNetV3-Large, were trained on the same photos for comparison.

      Started as a group coursework project at Bournemouth University and developed by Rollins Edeh.
